Who we are

We are a team of vets and animal-care staff who believe a clinic visit should leave you understanding more than when you walked in.

Every pet that comes through our door gets a digital health record: weight, diagnosis, treatment and the date of the next vaccine. Any member of our team can see the full history, so you never have to start your pet's story from the beginning.

We treat dogs, cats, birds and rabbits at the clinic, and visit farms for livestock by arrangement.
